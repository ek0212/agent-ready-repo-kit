# Writing Process

## Rules To Apply

- `rules/writing-constraints.md`
- `rules/static-text-constraints.md`
- `rules/structure-constraints.md`

## Inputs

- Draft text or writing request.
- Intended audience.
- Source material for factual claims.

## Procedure

### Step 1: Identify The Job

Classify the task as drafting, editing, summarizing, explaining, or auditing claims.

### Step 2: Structure The Content

Use MECE sections or bullets when organizing categories, options, plans, or tradeoffs. Name unknowns instead of hiding gaps.

### Step 3: Tighten The Prose

Make the text direct, concise, and plain. Remove banned patterns from the writing rules.

### Step 4: Check Durability And Claims

Remove stale or volatile static text. Cite factual claims or mark them as assumptions or inferences.

## Gotchas

- Do not add inspirational framing when the user needs a clear answer.
- Do not preserve a stale detail just because it sounds specific.

## Outputs

- Revised prose.
- Notes on unsourced claims, assumptions, or remaining uncertainty.
